About

About the founder and the brand

Hemshankar Agarwal leads Vasuki NFC with a straightforward goal: make business identity faster, smarter and more premium through NFC and digital-first products that actually solve real customer interaction problems.

Instead of selling disposable, forgettable print material, the focus is on building better ways for businesses to share contact details, websites, social links, review pages and menus. Vasuki NFC works at the intersection of physical products and digital experiences — where one tap or one scan should do the job cleanly.

Brand MissionTurn everyday business sharing into a premium tap-and-connect experience.
Primary MarketShops, professionals, restaurants, hotels, startups and local businesses that want better presentation and better conversion.
Core AdvantagePractical smart products backed by branding, setup support and clear use cases.

Operating Flow

How the founder approach works

No fluff. This is the practical sequence behind the business and product direction.

The founder role is not just "owner" text on a page. It means identifying actual customer pain points, packaging them into simple products, and then improving the whole flow from enquiry to usage.

Product ThinkingCustomer FlowBrand PresentationSupport
01 · Spot the frictionFind where business owners are wasting time in sharing details, collecting reviews or presenting themselves.
02 · Convert it into a productTurn the problem into an NFC card, smart profile, QR tool or review product with a clear use case.
03 · Make it usableKeep the experience simple enough that customers can actually use it without confusion.
04 · Improve the presentationPremium look matters. A good product that looks cheap still underperforms.
